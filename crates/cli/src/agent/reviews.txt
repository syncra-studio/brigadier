brigadier help reviews: adversarial reviews, in proportion. A small change with its result shown lands without a review.

- Plan review, before a big job goes to workers: `brigadier start --reviewing session "<the plan>"`.
- End check, once, on the merged tree, never mid-run: `brigadier start --reviewing session "<the job and its done-when>"`, or `--reviewing <id>` for one worker's commits. The reviewer tries to break it, runs every "done when" and reports pass or fail with its output.
- It runs on another agent's Best model than the author's, else a fresh session of the author's agent. It gets the task, its done-when and the commits, never the author's summary, and commits nothing. Its findings come to you as its result.
- You triage: fix the valid findings through workers, and note each rejected one with its reason. One review, one fix pass, then report what is left; never loop.
- With Settings › Routing's Adversarial review on, each worker that ends with commits is reviewed and gets one turn to fix before its result comes to you, with a line saying how the review went.
